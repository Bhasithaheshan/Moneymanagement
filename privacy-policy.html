Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Option Trading Money Management ("we", "our", "the site") handles information when you use this website and tool.

1. Information We Collect

This tool is designed to run entirely in your browser. The trading data you enter — capital, targets, stop-loss marks, withdrawal amounts, and theme preference — is stored locally on your own device using your browser's localStorage. We do not transmit this data to any server, and we cannot see or access it.

We do not require account creation, and we do not knowingly collect names, emails, or other personal identifiers through the tool itself.

2. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This site may use cookies or similar technologies for the following purposes:

  • Remembering your display preferences (such as light/dark theme)
  • Serving advertisements through third-party ad networks (such as Google AdSense), where enabled
  • Basic, aggregated analytics to understand how the site is used

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

3. Third-Party Services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and, where used, libraries for exporting your data as an image, PDF, or spreadsheet. These requests may be visible to those third-party providers as standard web requests (such as your IP address and browser type), governed by their own privacy policies.

If advertising is enabled on this site, Google AdSense and its partners may collect and use data as described in the Google Advertising Privacy & Terms.

4. Data You Export

When you use the Image, PDF, or Excel export features, a file is generated and downloaded directly to your device. This file is never uploaded to or stored on our servers.

5. Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Your Choices

You can clear all locally stored data at any time using the "Reset" button within the tool, or by clearing your browser's site data for this domain.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via the Contact page.

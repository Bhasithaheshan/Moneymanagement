About Us

Money management, made visible

Most traders don't lose money because they can't find good setups — they lose it because they don't stick to a plan. Option Trading Money Management was built around one simple idea: if your daily target, stop-loss, and withdrawal rules are written down and tracked automatically, you're far more likely to actually follow them.

What This Tool Does

This is a free, browser-based calculator that turns a simple set of rules into a day-by-day, week-by-week schedule:

  • Set a daily profit target and a hard stop-loss as a percentage of your capital
  • Mark each trading day as "Target Hit" or "Stop Loss" as it actually happens
  • Watch your capital compound automatically, day over day
  • Set a weekly withdrawal percentage so profits don't just stay "on paper"
  • Export your schedule as an image, PDF, or spreadsheet for your own records

Why We Built It

Discipline is easy to talk about and hard to practice — especially after a losing day tempts you to "make it back," or a winning day tempts you to push for more. A visible, pre-committed plan removes some of that emotional decision-making in the moment. That's the entire philosophy behind this tool: plan it before you trade, then just follow the plan.

Who It's For

This tool is built for retail traders working with options, CFDs, or similar short-term instruments who want a lightweight way to structure their risk and withdrawal habits — without spreadsheets or subscriptions.

What This Tool Is Not

It is not a signal service, not a broker, and not financial advice. It doesn't watch the markets or tell you when to trade. It only does the math on the numbers you give it. Please read our Risk Disclaimer before using it.
